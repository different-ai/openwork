# task-suggestion-card-alignment — Suggestion card titles line up across each row

This user-facing proof drives the OpenWork desktop app over CDP and measures the starter suggestion cards on the new task screen at three window widths, showing that both cards in a row start their title on the same line even when one card's description wraps onto a second line.

1. Opening OpenWork lands on the new task screen, and under the prompt box sit four starter suggestions in two rows of two. Each row stretches its two cards to the same height, but the descriptions are not the same length, so one of them wraps onto a second line while its neighbour stays on one. Both headings still start on exactly the same line, flush with the top of their card.

2. Dragging the window narrower squeezes the cards and re-wraps the descriptions underneath the headings. The pair of cards still matches in height, the amount of text inside them still does not match, and the two headings still read as one row.

3. Narrowing further tucks the sidebar away and gives the cards a different shape again. Even here every heading stays pinned to the top of its own card, so no title drifts toward the middle while its neighbour stays put.
