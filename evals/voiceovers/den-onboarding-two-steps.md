# den-onboarding-two-steps — Cloud setup is install the app, then turn on a model

1. A brand new workspace opens setup, and there are only two things to do: install the desktop app, then turn on a model. No marketplace to stock, no four-step checklist to work through.

2. Step one is the same workspace installer the team already uses everywhere else — it hands out a preconfigured OpenWork. If you already have the app, one tap marks the step done, and it stays done when you come back.

3. Step two gives two honest ways to get a model. OpenWork Models is the recommended path and needs no keys at all. Or bring your own key, and the logos show these are the providers you are probably already paying for.

4. Taking the recommended path lands straight on the workspace's models page, where the owner switches inference on for everyone.
