# org-delete-danger-zone — Organization deletion is explicit and complete

1. The owner is on General settings, scrolled to the bottom, where a red danger zone names the organization and keeps Delete organization separate from everyday settings.

2. The delete dialog asks for the organization name. Before the name is typed, the final button is locked; once the name matches, the destructive action is clearly armed.

3. After confirmation, the owner is back at the create-organization page. The old team name is gone, with no trace of the deleted organization left on screen.

4. In the platform admin panel, a real user account is searched, expanded, and deleted. The row disappears without the old invalid-id error.
