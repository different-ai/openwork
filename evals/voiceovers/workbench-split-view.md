# workbench-split-view — Arc-style split view from the tab strip

1. I have two sessions open as tabs. I right-click the one I want beside my current work and choose "New Split View with Current Tab" — no hunting for icons, the option is right where I clicked.

2. Both sessions now sit side by side, and the tab strip shows them as one joined pill — two segments, one per pane, each with its own close button.

3. The divider between the panes is mine to move: I drag it and the panes resize to how I want to work.

4. Closing one segment of the pill dissolves the split — I'm back to a single pane, and both sessions stay open as regular tabs.
