# Thirty-Second Reader

The reader decides in thirty seconds. Give them the story and stop.

**Why:** Long bodies, internal names and stale copies of CI output get skimmed or mistrusted. The spec, the report and the PR should tell one story in the same words.

- Lead with who can now do what, and what they saw before.
- Plain product words in titles, captions and release notes. No selectors, function names, or routing IDs.
- Do not copy verdicts, SHAs, or links that CI already posts; they go stale.
- Cut any section that makes the reader scroll.
- Every claim carries its evidence in the same sentence.
