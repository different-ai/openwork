A dashboard is a page of live, interactive apps in the desktop sidebar under **Dashboard**. It requires an OpenWork Cloud sign-in, and your deployment must have Dashboards turned on.

There are two ways to add apps:

| Path | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From your company | Admins | Assign MCP Apps from your org's Connectors to people or teams. See [Dashboards](/cloud/share-with-your-team/dashboards). |
| From chat | Any signed-in member, when available to your org | Ask OpenWork for an app or dashboard of a Workflow's results. Try the draft preview, ask for changes, then choose `Save` to add it to your dashboard. |

Apps built from chat display and explore results; they do not submit approvals or other writes.

To build your own MCP App widget, follow [Build an MCP App widget](/cloud/share-with-your-team/dashboards#build-an-mcp-app-widget).
