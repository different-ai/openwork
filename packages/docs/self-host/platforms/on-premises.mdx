OpenWork can be deployed inside your network using Linux VMs or Kubernetes. Your infrastructure can be a private cloud, a VMware or Proxmox environment, or physical servers. Choose the runtime first, then plan identity, networking, and data storage around it.

## Deployment approach

Use [Docker and VM planning](/self-host/platforms/docker-and-vms) for Linux hosts, or adapt the published [Helm chart](https://github.com/different-ai/openwork/tree/dev/packaging/helm/openwork-ee) to your cluster. This page is a requirements guide, not certification of every hypervisor or private-cloud combination.

## What you need

- Compatible container hosts or a Kubernetes cluster, with approved image access or an internal registry mirror.
- A private MySQL 8-compatible database, verified TLS, encrypted storage and backups, and a restore plan.
- Internal web/API DNS names, certificates trusted by member devices, and VPN or private-network access as needed.
- Secrets management, a migration procedure, and a secure first-administrator setup.
- Approved model-provider and connector access, or alternatives that work within your network constraints.
- Isolated worker runtimes and durable storage if cloud workspaces are required.

Follow [Private network deployment](/start-here/private-network-deployment) and [Certificate trust and proxies](/start-here/certificate-trust-and-proxies). Full isolation needs the separate [air-gapped checklist](/start-here/air-gapped-deployment); an internal install is not automatically air-gapped.
