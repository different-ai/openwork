**Privacy-first, open-source alternative to Claude Cowork.**

OpenWork lets you use 150+ LLMs, MCPs, Agents, Skills, and share your setups seamlessly with your team.

## Start

Once you have installed the [app](https://openworklabs.com/download), connect your provider of choice and send your first message. 

Below are some of the usual starting points:

- [Connect a provider](/start-here/connect-your-stack/sign-in-with-chatgpt)
- [Add an MCP](/start-here/connect-your-stack/add-an-mcp-server)
- [Control the browser](/start-here/do-work-with-it/control-the-browser)
- [Share a setup](/start-here/do-work-with-it/share-your-setup)

## For teams

[OpenWork Cloud →](/cloud/get-started) — shared workspaces, org skill hubs, managed providers, RBAC.

## Self-hosting

You can get started with [Self-hosting](/start-here/self-host) here. If you're an enterprise and want support in a private deployment or during self-hosting, check out [Enterprise](/cloud/enterprise) or [book a call directly](https://cal.com/team/openwork/enterprise).
