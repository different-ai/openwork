<Note>
**Local or managed.** Local workspace skills remain free and require no OpenWork Cloud sign-in; copy their files yourself when you only need an unmanaged handoff. For centrally assigned team skills and updates, publish a plugin through an OpenWork Cloud [Collection](/cloud/share-with-your-team/collections).
</Note>

OpenWork no longer shares skills from a `Workspace > Skills` screen. The desktop app shows what is already available to your agent.

## Find shared skills

1. Open `Settings > Library` in the desktop app.
2. Use the `Skills` filter or search to find skills from your workspace and organization.
3. Click a skill and choose `View details` to read its description; local skills can be revealed or removed, while OpenWork Connect skills are read-only.
4. Click `Refresh` if an admin just updated a Collection or changed your access.
5. To publish or customize a skill for teammates, follow [Publish and copy a skill](/start-here/do-work-with-it/publish-and-copy-a-skill). For larger setups with several plugins or audiences, see [Collections](/cloud/share-with-your-team/collections).
