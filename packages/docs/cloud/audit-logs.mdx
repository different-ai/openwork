**Observability › Audit logs** in OpenWork Cloud lists the changes made in your
organization: who made each one, when, from where, and whether it worked.

## What's recorded

OpenWork groups everything one action did into an **operation**. For example,
removing a member is one operation, even if it also revokes that person's API
keys. Each operation shows who did it, what they did, and the result.

Recorded changes include:

- **Members and invitations**: invitations created, refreshed, canceled or
  rejected; role changes; removals; ownership transfers.
- **Roles and permissions**: roles and permission sets created, changed or
  deleted, and which teams they apply to.
- **API keys**: keys created, deleted, or revoked when the owner's access
  changed.
- **Single sign-on and SCIM**: SSO connections registered, turned on or off,
  or deleted; SCIM tokens rotated, group mapping changes, and reconciles.
- **Sign-in and accounts**: sign-ins, failed sign-ins, revoked sessions,
  password and email changes, and OAuth tokens issued or revoked.
- **Agent permissions** and **allowed web origins**.
- **Requests to OpenWork's API**: which route was called, by whom, and the
  result. Request bodies, query strings, headers and IP addresses are never
  kept.

The **Event type** filter on the page lists every event type your installation
records.

Secrets are never recorded: no bearer tokens, API key secrets, SCIM tokens,
SAML certificates or OIDC client secrets. Where a change touches credentials,
the entry shows that they changed, not their values.

For the structured `[audit-alert]` log lines operators can route to alerting,
see [Security and operations](/cloud/security-and-operations#audit-events).

## Find an entry

At the top of the page:

- **From (local time)** and **To (local time)**: a date range.
- **Event type**: one kind of change, or **All event types**.
- **Search IDs**: an exact operation, event, request or resource ID.

Open **More filters** for:

- **Actor**: who did it.
- **Result**: **Running**, **Succeeded**, **Failed**, **Partially completed**
  or **Unknown**.
- **Origin**: **API**, **Cloud UI**, **Agent (MCP)**, **Scheduler**,
  **Webhook** or **Platform admin**.

Click **Apply filters**. **Clear filters** shows everything again. Click
**Load more operations** to see older entries, and **Refresh history** for the
newest.

## See what changed

Click an entry to open its timeline. Each step shows the fields that changed,
with their values **Before** and **After**. Open **Technical details** for the
operation and actor IDs, the origin, and how many events were recorded.

## Who can see it

You need the **View audit history** permission to read audit logs. Admins have
it by default. Without it, **Audit logs** shows **Locked** in the sidebar. See
[Members and roles](/cloud/members-and-rbac).

Turning recording on or off needs the **Change audit capture** permission.
Admins have it by default, and it asks for a recent sign-in.

## Plans and recording

Recording new activity is part of the **Enterprise** plan. On a self-hosted
installation, your operator can turn it on for every organization with the
`DEN_AUDIT_SELF_HOSTED_ENABLED=true` setting on Den API.

Reading history doesn't depend on your plan. If your organization stops being
on Enterprise, everything recorded so far stays visible; only new activity
stops being recorded.

### Capture and storage

Open **Capture and storage** at the bottom of the page to see:

- whether recording is **Available** on your plan
- the **Capture audit logs** switch for your organization (on by default)
- **Effective recording**: **Recording** or **Not recording**
- **Retained operations**, **Recorded events** and **Oldest available history**

The totals are recalculated once a day. On a self-hosted installation, that
needs the scheduled *audit usage refresh* job; see
[Scheduled maintenance jobs](/self-host/deploy-to-your-cloud/overview#scheduled-maintenance-jobs).

## How long it's kept

OpenWork doesn't delete audit history today. **Capture and storage** shows a
planned limit on retained operations under **Technical details**, but cleanup
only runs as a dry run: nothing is deleted.

## Export

People with **View audit history** can export audit history through the API
with `GET /v1/audit/export`, as NDJSON (the default) or CSV. Each response is
one page. Keep requesting with the `X-Audit-Next-Cursor` header value until it
no longer appears. Use the same filters as the page. Send an
[API key](/cloud/api-keys) in the `x-api-key` header to call it from a script.

CSV pages contain summary fields only, without before and after values.
