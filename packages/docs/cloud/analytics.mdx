**Observability › Analytics** in OpenWork Cloud shows how your team uses
OpenWork. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Usage & adoption** | Active people, sessions, tasks and the models people pick | Enterprise |
| **Plugins & connectors** | How often each plugin, skill and connector is used, by whom, and what fails | Every plan |
| **Workflow Runs** | Your saved workflow and one-off task runs, and whether each one worked | Enterprise |

Analytics only counts things. It never includes prompts, code, file contents,
diffs, secrets or terminal output.

## Who can see it

You need the **View usage analytics** permission. Admins have it by default.
Without it, **Analytics** doesn't appear in the sidebar. See
[Members and roles](/cloud/members-and-rbac).

On OpenWork Cloud, **Usage & adoption** and **Workflow Runs** are part of the
Enterprise plan. On other plans, **Usage & adoption** shows an Enterprise notice
instead of numbers, and the **Workflow Runs** tab doesn't appear. Usage is
still recorded, so it shows up as soon as the plan includes it. See
[Pricing and licensing](/start-here/pricing-and-licensing).

<Note>
  Numbers appear once members sign in to the OpenWork app with your
  organization and run tasks. Until then, the page shows **No activity yet**.
  See [Sign in from the desktop app](/cloud/get-started#sign-in-from-the-desktop-app).
</Note>

## Usage and adoption

The **Usage & adoption** tab answers "is my team using OpenWork, and does it
work for them?"

- **OpenWork users**: people in the organization, and how many invitations are
  still pending.
- **Active this week**: people with at least one event in the last 7 days, and
  how many were active in the last 30 days.
- **Sessions this week**: sessions in the last 7 days, and in the last 30 days.
- **Tasks this week**: tasks in the last 7 days, and their success rate.

Below the totals, charts show active users, sessions and tasks (completed and
failed) over time. Choose **Daily** for the last 30 days or **Weekly** for the
last 12 weeks. Days are counted in UTC.

The **Models** section lists which models people used in the last 30 days, and
how many sessions used the **Default model** versus a **Manually selected** one.
The last row shows **Avg task duration**, **Tasks completed** and **Tasks
failed** over 30 days.

### Look at one project

Use the project filter (**All projects** by default) to narrow the numbers to
one project, for example to follow a rollout. **OpenWork users** stays the
organization total.

Click **Refresh analytics** to load the latest numbers.

## Plugins and connectors

The **Plugins & connectors** tab shows which parts of your Library people
actually use. It's available on every plan.

1. Choose **Plugins**, **Skills** or **Connectors**.
2. Choose a time range: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.

At the top you see how many items are **In use**, total **Uses**, **Failed**
uses (plugins and connectors only), and how many are **Not used**. Filter the
list to **All**, **Not used** or **Failing**, or search by name.

What counts as one use:

- **Skills**: each time an agent loads the skill.
- **Plugins**: their skills' loads plus their workflows' runs. **Failed**
  counts failed runs.
- **Connectors**: each tool call made through OpenWork. **Failed** counts
  calls that failed.

Click an item to see **Uses per day**, who uses it and how often, and what
started the uses (**Agents**, **Workflows**, **Automations**, **Apps** or
**Live views**). From there, click **Open in Library** or **Open connector** to
change it. Click a person to see everything they used.

If counting started partway through the time range, the page says **Counting
since** and the date.

Use this tab to find skills nobody uses, and connectors that fail before people
give up on them.

## Workflow runs

The **Workflow Runs** tab lists your own runs of saved workflows and one-off
tasks, newest first. Each run shows its name (or **One-off task**), when it
finished, and **Succeeded** or **Failed**.

Click **Details** on a run to see:

- the workflow's steps, when the run came from a saved workflow
- under **Technical details**: where the run came from (**Source**), the
  **Tool calls** it made, its **Duration**, and the **Error** if it failed

Runs come from OpenWork, [Automations](/start-here/do-work-with-it/workflows),
and AI tools connected through the [OpenWork MCP gateway](/start-here/connect-openwork-mcp).
The list refreshes every 30 seconds.
