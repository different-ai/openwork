**Settings → Auth & Security** is where an organization connects OpenWork to
its identity provider. It has two tabs:

- **Single sign-on (SSO)**: people sign in with your company account instead of
  a separate OpenWork password.
- **User provisioning (SCIM)**: people and groups are added to and removed from
  OpenWork when they change in your identity provider.

<Note>
Older OpenWork versions, and organizations where Auth & Security is not turned
on yet, show these as two separate pages: **Settings → SSO** and
**Settings → SCIM**. Everything else on this page is the same. Links to the old
pages open the matching Auth & Security tab.
</Note>

## Who can use it

| | Plan | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single sign-on | Team and Enterprise | The organization owner, or anyone granted **Manage single sign-on** |
| User provisioning | Enterprise | The organization owner, or anyone granted **Manage SCIM provisioning** |

On a plan without the feature, the tab says which plan includes it. People who
can manage billing see **Upgrade to Team** (single sign-on) or **Talk to us
about Enterprise** (user provisioning). See
[Pricing and licensing](/start-here/pricing-and-licensing).

## Single sign-on

Single sign-on works with any SAML or OIDC identity provider. Follow the guide
for yours:

- [Okta SAML SSO](/cloud/sso-okta)
- [Microsoft Entra SAML SSO](/cloud/sso-microsoft-entra)
- [Google Workspace SAML SSO](/cloud/sso-google-workspace)

Every connection goes through the same steps before anyone can use it:

1. Open **Settings → Auth & Security → Single sign-on (SSO)** and save your
   identity provider's details.
2. Verify your email domain with a DNS TXT record.
3. Run a real test sign-in.
4. Turn SSO on.

Until all four are done, the connection stays off and people keep signing in
the way they do today. Someone from your verified domain who signs in with SSO
for the first time joins the organization as a **Member**.

Once SSO is on, people at your verified domain see **Continue with SSO** first
when they sign in. To make SSO the only way into your organization, turn on
**Require SSO** (Enterprise plan). Depending on your organization's settings,
this switch is at the top of the **Single sign-on (SSO)** tab or under
**Authentication** in **Settings → General**. Where available, **Restrict this
domain to our SSO** also stops people at your domain from using a password or
Google anywhere in OpenWork, and **Ask for SSO again** sets how often people
must sign in with SSO again.

## User provisioning

User provisioning (SCIM) keeps OpenWork in step with your identity provider:
people you assign get access, and people you remove or deactivate lose it.
You can also create OpenWork teams from your identity provider's groups.

User provisioning needs single sign-on turned on first. Then follow the guide
for your identity provider:

- [Okta SCIM provisioning](/cloud/scim-okta)
- [Microsoft Entra SCIM provisioning](/cloud/scim-microsoft-entra)

Google Workspace can be used for single sign-on but not for user provisioning;
see [Google Workspace SCIM provisioning](/cloud/scim-google-workspace).

In **Settings → Auth & Security → User provisioning (SCIM)**, create the
connector, then give your identity provider the **SCIM base URL** and the
bearer token shown once after you create or rotate it. Failed syncs are retried
and listed on the same tab until they are resolved.
