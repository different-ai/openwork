The **Users & Teams** tab answers one question: who can use which models, how
much they can spend, and how much they've spent. You change access on each
provider; this tab shows the result for every person and team in one place.

## Where to find it

In OpenWork Cloud, open **AI Gateway** and select the **Users & Teams** tab.

Seeing it needs three permissions: **View Gateway providers**, **View Gateway
usage**, and **View usage limits**. Organization owners and admins have them by
default.

## What the tab shows

At the top, a row for **Everyone in** your organization shows what every member
gets, including people who join later.

Below it, switch between **People** and **Teams**, or use **Filter by name**.
Each row shows:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Can use** | The providers whose models this person or team can use. **Nothing yet** means none. |
| **Limit** | Their spend limit and where it comes from, or **No limit**. |
| **Last 31 days** | Their estimated spend. |

Spend is an estimate from public model prices, not your provider's bill. See
[Token costs](/ai-gateway/token-costs).

### Open a team

Click a team to see only its members. A strip at the top shows the team's
providers, limit, and spend, with **Set a team limit** or **Edit team limit**.

No teams yet? Click **Create a team**. Teams are managed in **Members**.

### Open a person

Click a person to see:

- **Last 31 days**: their total estimated spend.
- **What they can use**: each provider they can use. Click **Change in AI
  Providers** to change it.
- **Spend limit**: whether they're **Within the limit**, **Over the limit**, or
  **Paused**, with **Set a limit** or **Edit**.
- **Models they use**: spend per model.

## Give people models

Access is set on each provider, not on the person. To give someone models:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider">
    Go to **AI Gateway › AI Providers** and click the provider. To add a new one,
    click **Add a provider**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use it">
    Under **Who can use it**, turn on **Everyone in the organization**, or turn
    it off and click **Add person** or **Add team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which models">
    Under **Models**, pick **All** models (for example **All Anthropic models**), or **Only the ones I
    pick** and select them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. Members' apps pick up the new models on their next
    sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A person can use a provider's models if any route gives them access: everyone,
one of their teams, or them directly.

[OpenWork Models](/ai-gateway/openwork-models) is different: when it's on,
everyone in the organization can use it.

## Shared key or each person's own account

Each provider has a **Key** setting:

- **Shared API key**: you paste one key, and everyone with access uses it. The
  key stays on OpenWork's servers; members never see it.
- **Each member signs in**: each person connects their own account, such as
  Google sign-in for [Vertex AI](/ai-gateway/google-agent-platform), AWS IAM
  Identity Center for [Amazon Bedrock](/ai-gateway/amazon-bedrock), Microsoft
  Entra ID for [Microsoft Foundry](/ai-gateway/microsoft-foundry), or a personal
  key for [LiteLLM](/ai-gateway/litellm). Only some providers support this.

## Stop members from adding their own keys

On **AI Gateway › AI Providers**, the **Who can use models** row shows your
organization's policy. Click **Change**:

- **Only models you provide**: members see only what you set up in AI Gateway
  and OpenWork Models, and can't add their own provider keys. You can still
  allow **Admins may still add their own keys on their device**.
- **Any model**: members can also add their own provider keys on their device.
  Your AI Gateway models still show up.

The same dialog has **Free starter model (Auto)**, which lets members fall back
to OpenWork's free, rate-limited model. Changes apply to every member, on
desktop and the web, the next time they open OpenWork.

## Set limits

To cap spend for everyone, a team, or a person, see [Limits](/ai-gateway/limits).
The **Limit** column on this tab shows which limit applies to each row.

## Model groups, credential sets and access grants

You may see these terms in permissions, the API, or a member's **Technical
details** in My Library. They're the pieces behind the screens above:

- A **model group** is a set of models from one provider: all of them, or only
  the ones you picked.
- A **credential set** is how those models are paid for: the shared API key, or
  each member's own sign-in.
- An **access grant** gives a model group to everyone in the organization, a
  team, or one person.

The **Who can use it**, **Models** and **Key** sections on a provider set all
three for you.
