Connect Slack once for your organization, and OpenWork can search, read and post in Slack as each person who signs in. You don't need any bot tokens or code.

## Before you start

- You're an **owner or admin** in OpenWork Cloud.
- You can **create apps in your Slack workspace**. If your workspace blocks app installs, a Slack admin needs to approve the app.
- Allow about five minutes. You'll switch between OpenWork Cloud and Slack's app settings.

Not an admin? Ask one to follow this page, then skip to [Everyone signs in](#everyone-signs-in).

## 1. Start the setup

Start from either place:

- **In the desktop app:** open **Library › Add to library › Connector**, pick **Slack**, then click **Set up in OpenWork Cloud**.
- **In OpenWork Cloud:** open **Manage › Connectors › Add a connector**, then pick **Slack**.

![Set up Slack in the desktop app, with the Set up in OpenWork Cloud button](/images/slack-connect/desktop-set-up-slack.png)

Either way, the **Add Slack** checklist opens in OpenWork Cloud.

## 2. Choose what OpenWork can do

Pick one option. Nothing is selected for you.

- **Read and act** (recommended): searches and reads messages, files and canvases, and posts, reacts and creates channels.
- **Read only**: searches and reads, but can't post or change anything in Slack.

OpenWork always acts as the person who signed in, and only sees what that person can already see.

![The Add Slack checklist in OpenWork Cloud](/images/slack-connect/cloud-choose-access.png)

## 3. Create the Slack app

1. Click **Create app in Slack**. Slack opens **Create from a manifest** with the redirect URL, scopes and MCP setting already filled in.
2. Under **Workspace**, pick your Slack workspace, then click **Next**.
3. Review the app and click **Create and Install**.
4. Click **Allow** to approve the app in your workspace.

![Slack's Review your app step](/images/slack-connect/slack-review-app.png)

Already have a Slack app you want to use? Open **Use an existing Slack app** in the checklist. It has the redirect URL, scopes and manifest to copy into that app.

## 4. Add the client ID and secret

1. In Slack, click **Go to App Settings**. **Basic Information › App Credentials** shows the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
2. In Slack, open **Agents** and check that **Slack Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server** is on. Apps created from the manifest have it on already.
3. Back in the **Add Slack** checklist, turn on **MCP is on in my Slack app**.
4. Paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret**, then click **Save changes**.

Keep the client secret out of chats, screenshots and source control.

![The checklist with every step done except Sign in with Slack](/images/slack-connect/cloud-ready-to-sign-in.png)

## 5. Sign in to test

1. Click **Sign in with Slack** and approve with your own Slack account.
2. When Slack shows **You're connected**, close that tab and go back to OpenWork Cloud.
3. The page should say **Slack passed all 5 checks**. If a check fails, it explains what to fix and offers **Check again**.

## 6. Choose who can use it and add Slack

1. Under **How people connect**, keep **Each person signs in** so everyone uses their own Slack account.
2. Under **Who can use it**, turn on **Everyone in the organization**, or add specific people and teams.
3. Click **Add Slack**.

![Slack passed all 5 checks, with sharing options](/images/slack-connect/cloud-checks-passed.png)

## Everyone signs in

Each person connects their own Slack account once:

- **In the desktop app:** open **Library**. Slack appears under **From OpenWork**. Click **Sign in with Slack** if it asks.
- **On the web:** open **Your Connections** and connect Slack.

Then try it. Ask something like *"Post a message in #general that says hello from OpenWork"* or *"Summarize what happened in #launch this week."*

![OpenWork posting a message in Slack](/images/slack-connect/desktop-post-in-slack.png)

## Common questions

**The desktop app still says "Not set up for your organization."**
Go back to the **Library**. Slack shows as ready there once setup finishes in OpenWork Cloud.

**Sign-in fails with "The MCP server refused to start a session (HTTP 400)."**
In Slack, open the app whose client ID you saved, go to **Agents**, and turn on **MCP**. Then click **Check again**. See [Slack connection troubleshooting](/cloud/share-with-your-team/shared-mcp-connections#troubleshooting-slack).

**I don't see the checklist, only the client ID and secret fields.**
Your OpenWork may not have guided setup turned on yet (for example, a self-hosted install). Follow the [manual Slack steps](/cloud/share-with-your-team/shared-mcp-connections#slack) instead.

**Which scopes does OpenWork ask for?**
**Read and act** asks for all 30 user scopes Slack's MCP server offers. **Read only** asks for the same list without any `:write` scope. See [Slack scopes](/cloud/share-with-your-team/shared-mcp-connections#slack-scopes).
